# Process Record Guidance

Read this reference for new identity work, historical reconstruction, or a reusable handoff. The goal is traceability, not paperwork: preserve the decisions and evidence another person or agent would need to understand, reproduce, govern, or safely extend the brand.

## Separate evidence from reconstruction

Classify each material statement as one of:

- **Evidence:** directly supported by a file, commit, message, dated approval, rendered output, or other inspectable source.
- **Inference:** a reasonable interpretation derived from evidence; record the supporting source and label the conclusion.
- **Unknown:** not established by available material. Do not fill the gap with a plausible narrative.

For an existing brand, inspect source artwork, asset documentation, version history, implemented metadata, and reachable design records. A final asset pack can establish what was approved and delivered, but not necessarily how rejected directions evolved or what brand strategy originally motivated them.

## Establish only the brand foundation the assignment needs

For a new identity, repositioning, or a visual direction that depends on unresolved strategy, capture enough strategic context to make defensible design decisions:

- audience and positioning;
- promise or value proposition;
- differentiators and relevant category associations;
- brand personality and tone cues;
- relevant values and explicit non-values or exclusions;
- target markets, languages, and scripts;
- intended surfaces and success criteria.

Do not turn an asset-production request for an already approved identity into a strategy workshop. Reuse established project evidence and mark missing historical strategy as unknown when it does not block the requested production or review work.

## Maintain two records

Use the templates only when the project has no equivalent records:

- [Brand process log](../assets/templates/brand-process-log.md): brief, foundation, evidence register, explored directions, decisions, approvals, clearance status, governance exceptions, and open questions.
- [Brand handoff](../assets/templates/brand-handoff.md): approved master, invariants, asset inventory, implementation state, machine-readable delivery, QA evidence, lifecycle status, and limitations.

Store records in the project's existing documentation location. If no convention exists, keep them beside the brand source files or in a clearly named brand documentation directory. Do not copy the templates into a project unless the brand task authorizes creating durable project artifacts.

## Record decisions at checkpoints

Update the process log when a choice materially narrows or changes the identity:

- minimum brand foundation or brief approval when one is needed;
- concept direction selection or rejection;
- palette, type, geometry, negative-space, localization, or lockup approval;
- production master freeze;
- asset-pack or machine-readable handoff acceptance;
- implementation and visual/accessibility acceptance.

Capture the date, decision maker when known, evidence location, decision, and short rationale. Do not claim approval from silence, a generated preview, or an unverified implementation.

## Track clearance and governance without overstating certainty

For naming or visual similarity work, record one of these statuses when relevant: `not requested`, `preliminary screen`, `specialist/legal review recorded`, or `unknown`. Include the jurisdiction, trademark classes or other scope, source/database, search or review date, and limitations when known. A preliminary similarity search is risk screening, not legal clearance.

Record approved exceptions and lifecycle changes instead of silently replacing assets. When useful, capture the owner, current status, version or stable revision, deprecation date, replacement asset, locale/market scope, usage-rights constraints, review date, or expiry date. Keep superseded files distinguishable from current approved assets.

## Preserve source relationships

The handoff should identify the editable master and explain how derivatives were produced. Keep concept images, production sources, exported assets, design tokens or manifests, and implementation files distinct. Machine-readable files should point back to approved sources and versions rather than becoming an undocumented parallel source of truth.

Record font names, script coverage, fallback behavior, and licensing constraints without embedding unlicensed font files. Treat localized wordmarks and script-specific lockups as separately approved variants.

When a repository is available, include stable paths and relevant commits or tags. For external design tools, record the document or frame identifier only when it is safe and accessible to the intended maintainers.
