# Evidence Authority Model

Apply scope eligibility first, then this authority order:

```text
WRITTEN_CANON > CANONICAL_VISUAL > APPROVED_CALIBRATION
> ROLE_SCOPED_EXTERNAL_REFERENCE > ACCEPTED_CONTINUITY
> PREVIEW_ONLY > GENERATED_UNVERIFIED
```

## Scope before rank

Written Canon governs fixed invariants. Canonical visuals support their declared identity/structure regions. Approved calibration supplies only its declared view and regions: visible but unauthorized regions in that image have no authority. A diagnostic board is not automatically generation evidence.

External references control only assigned non-primary-identity dimensions. Accepted continuity corroborates accepted series state but cannot replace Canon. Preview evidence controls selected shot geometry only. Unverified generation has no identity or continuity authority.

An approved transport representation inherits its source's authorized scope, not independent authority. A newer file, a larger image, or a successful prior generation never outranks Canon by recency or appearance.

## Operational roles are a separate axis

Resolve [image roles](image-role-resolver.md) before external routing:

```text
PROTECTED_CANONICAL > EDIT_TARGET > PREVIEW_SHOT_REFERENCE
> ACCEPTED_CONTINUITY > EXPLICIT_EXTERNAL_ROLE > ORIGINAL_PROMPT_REFERENCE
```

An accepted clean master being edited has operational role EDIT_TARGET while retaining its source provenance. This does not upgrade it to canonical identity evidence. Protected canonical assets are not ordinary edit targets; changing them requires a subject-owned maintenance task.

## Conflicts

Preserve higher-authority fixed invariants and current explicitly authorized operation semantics. Apply lower-authority evidence only in non-conflicting scope. An unresolved contradiction is `SPEC_UNRESOLVED`, not an invitation to blend identities or drop constraints.

External primary-identity promotion is outside ordinary routing. It requires a separate explicit Subject Canon change, with its own provenance. A preview, delivery file, failed result, or imported chat image must not be relabeled as canonical evidence to bypass a blocker.

Every shot using continuity still independently requires the canonical evidence selected by its Subject Pack profiles.
