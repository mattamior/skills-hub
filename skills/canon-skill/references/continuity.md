# Clean Master and Continuity

Continuity evidence supplements Canon; it never replaces Canon.

## Provenance

CONSTRUCTION_INTERMEDIATE is temporary generation/compositing/preparation output. CLEAN_MASTER_CANDIDATE is the exact post-finalization candidate under full validation. ACCEPTED_CLEAN_MASTER is an eligible unwatermarked final candidate that passed all required checks. DELIVERY_DERIVATIVE is an export/presentation file derived from a master.

Bind accepted records to subject and pack revision, source series/shot/revision, packet digest, result, exact candidate/validated checksums, hooks, actual inspection evidence and acceptance scope. Keep accepted pixels immutable; later edits produce new candidates and identifiers.

## Admission

Admit only a real, provenance-verified ACCEPTED_CLEAN_MASTER with matching candidate/validated hashes, compatible current subject/pack and the requested series. Declare admitted continuity dimensions and why they are needed. Enforce any pack cap and prefer none when unnecessary.

Never admit previews, diagnostic boards/closeouts, failed or HARD_RESET results, RETRY_REQUIRED output, REFINE_ELIGIBLE candidates, unverified history, construction intermediates, simulations, watermarked files or delivery-only derivatives. The helper validates record consistency, not whether a caller truthfully performed visual acceptance.

An independently generated and approved calibration master may have an explicitly declared eligible scope; decorative boards never inherit that status. Calibration authority promotion is separate from continuity and requires subject-owned approval.

## Future shots

Each next shot still selects its required Canon. A previous generated image is never the sole identity source. Accepted environment, wardrobe, objects, lighting or camera language may supplement the spec, but cannot overwrite written or primary visual invariants.

When a master is superseded, keep its audit record and reevaluate active relevance rather than promoting the newer image to stronger authority. Import across sessions only with explicit scope, usable images and verified provenance.

## Delivery

Resize, format conversion, watermarking, sharpening and presentation compositing produce derivatives, not new identity evidence. Prefer re-derivation from the master over repeatedly editing an already marked delivery file. A derivative-only task can complete as such but cannot enter final-master continuity.
