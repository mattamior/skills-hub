# Clean Master and Continuity

Continuity evidence supplements Canon; it never replaces Canon.

## Four provenance classes

`CONSTRUCTION_INTERMEDIATE` is a scaffold, temporary omission, mask/composite or processing stage that has not completed the final validation contract. It is not a master, identity authority or continuity source.

`CLEAN_MASTER_CANDIDATE` is the exact finalized image submitted for applicable validation. A refine-eligible candidate may be an explicit bounded edit target, but remains unaccepted. Preview and diagnostic artifacts keep their limited output kind even if they pass their own checks.

`ACCEPTED_CLEAN_MASTER` is an eligible candidate that passed all required validation, hooks and approval gates. Record subject/pack revision, shot/revision, packet digest, result/attempt, byte hash, evidence ids and validation/hook/approval provenance. Its accepted bytes are immutable; later edits create new candidates and new master ids.

`DELIVERY_DERIVATIVE` is an export, crop, conversion, watermark, composited presentation, sharpening or other delivery copy. Retain a source-master link, but never replace the master with it. A preview export remains preview-derived, not an accepted master.

## Admission

Admit only a verified accepted clean master relevant to the current subject, Canon revision and series property. Check exact source and byte provenance, not merely a filename or a visually convincing resemblance. A host that permits a reviewed cross-revision compatibility mapping must explicitly verify it; the optional Python helper conservatively requires an exact subject/pack revision match.

Admission is optional. Prefer no auxiliary when Canon and the effective spec already suffice. Honor subject-owned limits and selection priorities; do not stack every prior generation into the next call.

Continuity may carry accepted environment, wardrobe, objects, camera language, lighting, staging and other series state. Canonical appearance is corroborating only. A conflict with written Canon or canonical visual authority never elevates the continuity image.

## Prohibited inputs

Never admit previews, diagnostics, unverified history, construction intermediates, HARD_RESET or RETRY_REQUIRED results, REFINE_ELIGIBLE candidates, delivery-only copies or watermarked derivatives. Renaming or importing such an artifact does not change its authority.

Every later shot independently satisfies required canonical coverage. Valid execution is `Canon + optional accepted continuity -> next result`, never a progressively unanchored generated-image-only identity chain.

## Revisions and imports

Preserve prior accepted masters when later candidates fail. When a new revision is accepted, update the active pointer and reassess old auxiliary relevance without changing historical provenance or upgrading the new image to Canon.

Cross-session continuation requires explicit scoped SESSION_IMPORT, provenance checks and current-surface image materialization. Old gates, tool handles and remembered generated images do not automatically become executable state. A separate subject-owned approval process, not ordinary continuity, owns any future Canon update.
